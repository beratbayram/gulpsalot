# Ideas

Parked for later. "Seen in" names the game an idea comes from. Anything that changes
spawns or gameplay timing has to keep the daily challenge deterministic (see `AGENTS.md`).

# LATER

- **Run perks.** Every so many points, pick one of two perks for the rest of the run:
  longer reach, wider tip, slower drain, bee warning ping. Seen in Froguelike, where you
  pick new tongues and upgrades as you level up.
- **Daily mutators.** The daily picks a twist from the date: bee day, night only, golden
  hour (more golden flies), windy. Keeps the daily fresh without new content.
- **Seasons from the real date.** Falling leaves in autumn, snow on the pad in winter,
  blossoms in spring. Purely visual.
- **Shooting stars at night.** Rare; catching one grants a "wish" (a small boost or a
  secret unlock).
- **Bug journal.** A page per insect kind with a portrait, the lifetime count, the best
  single run and a short real fact. Entries open on first catch. Collection like Neko
  Atsume's catbook.
- **Visitors while away.** Open the game to find who came by since last time (a snail on
  the pad, a duck), depending on the decorations. No pressure, nothing to lose. Seen in
  Neko Atsume, where cats only visit while the game is closed.
- **Looks that change the world.** A few special outfits also restyle the pond (a pirate
  hat turns the pad into a raft, a wizard hat gives purple fireflies). Seen in Crossy Road.
- **Swipe shots (advanced).** A drag curves the rope through several insects. Seen in
  Mister Frog. Tap stays the default so the one-tap feel survives.
- **Lily pad hops.** Two or three pads; tapping an empty pad hops Pofik there, changing
  his angle and reach. A bigger change to the core loop, worth a prototype first.
- **Share image card.** The daily share sends a text line today; an image card rendered
  from the canvas would stand out more in chats.
- **Hold meta back on first launch.** Show the daily, wardrobe and achievements from the
  second session, so the first one is only the core loop. Common hyper-casual onboarding
  advice.
- **Online leaderboard** (needs a backend, for example Supabase).
- **Game Center and Play Games leaderboards** in the native builds, a backend-free option.
- **Electron desktop build** if a Steam or desktop release ever makes sense.
- **Web game portals** (Poki, CrazyGames) behind the existing monetization interface.
