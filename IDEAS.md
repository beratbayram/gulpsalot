# Ideas

Parked for later. The current build focuses on the core loop.

## Content

- **Golden fly.** Rare and fast, with a big score and hunger bonus, its own sparkle trail
  and sound.
- **Dragonfly.** Fast zig-zag flyer, harder to hit, worth more.
- **Multi-catch combos.** Several insects in one shot, or catches in quick succession,
  build a multiplier with escalating effects and pitch.
- **Day to night.** The sky slowly shifts from day to dusk to night as a run goes on;
  fireflies appear at night.
- **Pofik skins and hats** unlocked by best score.
- **Daily challenge.** A seeded run that is the same for everyone that day.
- **Achievements.** First bee dodge, 10-fly streak, survive two minutes, and so on.

## Platform and reach

- **Turkish and English** UI through a small i18n layer.
- **Online leaderboard** (needs a backend, for example Supabase).
- **Ambient music loop** and an optional CC0 sample pack for more natural sounds.
- **Electron desktop build** if a Steam or desktop release ever makes sense.
- **Web game portals** (Poki, CrazyGames) behind the existing monetization interface.
